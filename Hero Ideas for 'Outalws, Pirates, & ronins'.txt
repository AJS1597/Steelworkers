I've been pitching an idea since the beginning of the assignment, to add a sort of 'commander' or 'Hero' system similar to Hearthstone. For the most part, we've been focusing on creating the foundation of the game, but right now it feels like a good time to try and toss some ideas on how I think they should work.


The main idea I want going for Heroes, is that they don't attack the opponent directly, but mainly serve as a buff for the cards within a player's deck, or stand as an obstacle, perhaps to keep them balanced, once their ability is used up- they are immediately discarded, as to not overcomplicate the player. Think of it like a trump card, you use for situations where you need to dig yourself out of a bad spot.

No cost
One time use
Cannot affect the opponent directly, only their minions and their own.


These Heroes leave the fighting to the minions, while operating with skills under the battlefield.


Samurai
Miyamoto Masashi, "An undefeated duelist..."
When one of the opponent's minion is about to deal damage directly, this card activates to defeat the selected Minion. Only one Minion can be called out to a duel.



Date Masamune 'The iconic "One-Eyed Dragon," known for his crescent-moon helmet and aggressive campaigns in northern Japan.' 
When this card's ability is deployed, any minions that do not have synergy/ (ying-yang) are automatically attuned to the damage of the opponents minion Infront of them. 

Cowboys
Ned Kelly ' Infamous Armored Australian Bushranger' 
When this card is deployed on the board, the player's minions automatically get a +2 to armor for the duration of this turn. 

Wyatt Earp 'Legendary Lawman and central figure in the Gunfight at the O.K'
When this card is deployed onto the board, any card with the wrangle trait automatically draws 2, to the bounty/hire pool.

Pirates
Bartholomew Roberts, 'Often considered the most successful pirate of the era, capturing over 400 ships'
Any card defeated this round, will generate a bonus of 2 to the hire pool.


Blackbeard 'One of the most notorious captains of the Golden age of piracy, known for his thick black beard and theatrical intimidation tactics'. 
When this card is deployed, picking any minion that has taken damage previously is automatically scared off the player's board.


These are just a couple of ideas I wanted to pitch in, and use for later if anyone has any thoughts. They may not be the best- but it's some room for thought if we ever decide to play with the idea of Hero characters.
