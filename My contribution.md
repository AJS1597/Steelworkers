When your life drops to or below 15, 10, or five, you can draw a card from the special deck, which consists of five cards you choose before the game.
Special cards have no allegiances and can be used in any faction. Below are ten starting special cards for players to use.
Special cards are free to use.
Set 1: Grief
Denial- If a unit of yours would die, you may discard this card to instead leave it at one life.
Anger- Double the power of target unit.
Bargaining- Discard a card, then draw three cards.
Depression- Units your opponent controls have -1/0 this turn.
Acceptance- Remove a card in your opponent's discard pile from the game
Set 2: Elements
Metal- Gain 2 of your faction's resource.
Fire- When a unit you control deals damage to your opponent this turn, they discard a card, up to two cards max.
Earth- Give a unit +0/+3.
Wood- When a unit of yours dies, you may discard this to give another unit +2/+2.
Water- Whenever you discard a card, draw a card, up to two cards max.
