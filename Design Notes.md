1. No direct counter cards, general use cards only 
2. No direct type advantages, but picking from 3 teams at start to build your deck around.  
3. Decks should be a mixture of neutral and themed cards. 
4. Stronger cards, real life or semi-historical people involved in the groups (Nobunga, Blackbeard, Butch Cassidy). 
5. Decisions throughout & before game, player choice in deck building. 

Yin-Yang mechanic - Synergy (of sorts), need of each  unique to samurai deck) 

Armor - all damage taken is reduced by the armor amount

Duel mechanic - A minion with duel can block an unblocked minion (cowboy deck)

Wrangle - dealing damage causes the target to lose the wrangle value of attack

Plunder - A minion who has looting and deals damage to a player allows you to draw & then discard a card. 

Volley - apply adjacent units attack when dealing damage

Three separate alternate cost payment methods - one per deck type.  Cowboys, have looting gain bullets. Pirates, have bounty gain gold. Samurai, have Bushido gain honor.  Each has levels, for each level you gain one hire point specific to the deck.

Hire points - Two types, deck specific and standard. Deck specific can be used in place of standard hire points, but not the other way around.
