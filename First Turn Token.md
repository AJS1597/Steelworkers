My contribution is a new play element, a first turn token. This token is passed back and forth between both players after every round. It is assigned
first by rock, paper, and scissors.  This rebalances play, as going first is an inherent disadvantadge, so it keeps who goes first fresh each "round", with round here meaning draw
-> battle. This is easily tracked by a physical token, akin to a poker chip. This works to keep is easy to remember, functioning like the big/small blind in texas holdem. All it does is
signify which player puts down their cards first.
